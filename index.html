How to play

Reach the glowing beacon at the far end of each rooftop. Grab stars if you're feeling brave. Falling just sends you back to the last checkpoint — nobody runs out of lives here. With two players it's a race: first cape to the beacon wins the rooftop.

Player 1   AD move   W jump   S power
Player 2   ←→ move   ↑ jump   ↓ power
Phone, one player   move on the left, jump and power on the right
Phone, two players   P1 takes the left end, P2 the right
Esc pause   R restart
Turrets fire boxing gloves. They will not finish you off, but they will knock you clean off a ledge.
Inside a purple NO POWERS field, nobody's power works. Plain jumping only.
Pause any time to change hero or jump to another page.
